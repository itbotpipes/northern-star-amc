1. Privacy Policy

{{ COMPANY_NAME }} ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal and site-safety information you share with us when using our website, Customer Portal, and Fire Safety Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) services.

  • Contact & Account Details: Name, phone number, email address, company name, GSTIN, and site/building address.

  • To schedule site inspections, maintenance visits, refilling, and emergency fire safety support.

We never sell your personal data. Customer contact details are restricted to authorized operations/management staff; field technicians only see site address and technical scope required to perform inspections or repairs.
