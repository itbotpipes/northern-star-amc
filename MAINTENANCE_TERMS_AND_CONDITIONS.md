## 2. Complete Legal Terms & Conditions

### Clause 1. Scope of Service & Coverage
This Annual Maintenance Contract covers periodic inspection, testing, and routine servicing of the fire-safety equipment listed in this quotation/agreement, at the registered site only, for a period of twelve (12) months from the date of contract activation. Only equipment specifically listed at the time of activation is covered under this contract.

### Clause 2. Number of Scheduled Visits
The contract includes the agreed number of scheduled maintenance visits stated in the quotation, spaced evenly across the contract year (e.g., quarterly visits). Additional or emergency inspections requested outside this agreed schedule are chargeable.

### Clause 3. Emergency & Breakdown Calls
Reactive breakdown visits requested between scheduled visits are provided on a priority basis. Travel and technician labour for such emergency calls are covered under the contract; however, any spare parts, consumable refills, or replacement materials required are chargeable separately (see Clause 6).

### Clause 4. Included Services & Consumables
Routine maintenance consumables (minor cleaning supplies, lubricating agents, small fittings, testing consumables) and standard preventive servicing labour for the covered equipment are included in the AMC fee.

### Clause 5. Excluded Work & Infrastructure
Not included in the AMC fee:
1. Replacement of expired, damaged, worn-out, or non-functional parts.
2. Extinguisher refilling and mandatory hydrostatic pressure testing.
3. Replacement of gas cylinders, fire hoses, branch pipes, gauge glasses, or sprinkler heads.
4. Civil, electrical, or plumbing infrastructure (such as underground piping, building wiring, primary electrical panels, masonry, and water supply tanks).
5. Any equipment added to the premises after contract activation without prior written intimation to Northern Star Engineering.

### Clause 6. Spare Parts, Quotations & Liability Waiver
Where repair, overhaul, or replacement of parts is required, a separate material quotation is issued for the client's approval. The client may decline the recommendation; however, Northern Star Engineering bears zero liability for any fire incident, failure, penalty, or damage attributable to equipment the client has declined to repair or replace. A formal liability waiver is recorded upon decline.

### Clause 7. Site Access & Prerequisites
The client must ensure safe, unobstructed, and timely access to all equipment on the scheduled visit date, along with electricity, water supply, and necessary site keys or security clearance. If access is denied, obstructed, or the site is unready, that visit is treated as completed and will not be carried forward or refunded.

### Clause 8. Rescheduling Notice
Rescheduling requests must be communicated at least forty-eight (48) hours before the scheduled visit date. Northern Star Engineering will make reasonable efforts to reschedule to an alternative mutually agreed date within the active contract period.

### Clause 9. Old Equipment & Pre-Existing Defects
For equipment already installed (whether installed by Northern Star Engineering or third-party contractors), the AMC covers upkeep and routine servicing only. Any pre-existing defects, degraded components, or obsolescence identified during the initial inspection will be documented and reported to the client; rectification of such items is chargeable and outside the routine AMC fee.

### Clause 10. Warranties on Parts & Equipment
Warranties on any newly supplied equipment or replacement parts are strictly limited to the respective original equipment manufacturer's (OEM) warranty. The AMC does not extend, renew, or substitute OEM warranties.

### Clause 11. Standards & Statutory Fire NOC
All maintenance activities are executed in accordance with applicable Indian standards (National Building Code of India [NBC], Bureau of Indian Standards [BIS/IS], and relevant NFPA guidance). Statutory Fire NOC certification assistance is a distinct advisory service and remains subject to formal inspection and approval by the jurisdictional Fire Officer.

### Clause 12. Client Responsibilities
The client agrees to:
- Maintain equipment in a clean, accessible, and unhindered state between visits.
- Promptly report any system fault, leakage, pressure drop, or alarm condition to NSE.
- Not engage, permit, or authorize any third party to service, alter, or tamper with the covered equipment during the contract term.
- Operate all fire equipment strictly per standard operating procedures.

### Clause 13. Service Reports & Digital Acknowledgement
A digital service report is generated and issued upon completion of every visit. The client is requested to review and acknowledge the report. Digital records accessed and acknowledged via the customer portal or registered email constitute legally valid documentation of work performed.

### Clause 14. Payment Terms & GST
The annual AMC fee is payable within seven (7) days of contract activation (or as stated on the quotation). Services may be suspended if payment is not cleared when due. All charges are subject to statutory GST (Northern Star Engineering GST No. 24ALQPD0899P1ZD).

### Clause 15. Price Validity & Market Rates
This quotation is valid for the number of days specified thereon. Spare parts, consumables, piping, and metal item rates are subject to prevailing market prices at the time of actual replacement.

### Clause 16. Limitation of Liability
Northern Star Engineering's aggregate total liability under this contract, for any reason whatsoever, shall not exceed the annual AMC fee actually paid by the client. Northern Star Engineering is not liable for indirect, consequential, or economic loss, property damage, or bodily injury where the client has been notified of a defect and did not authorize timely repair or replacement.

### Clause 17. Contract Renewal & Termination
The contract is valid for twelve (12) months from activation and does not auto-renew. Either party may terminate the contract with thirty (30) days' prior written notice. A pro-rata refund is payable only for scheduled visits not yet performed. No refund is due after all scheduled visits have been completed.

### Clause 18. Governing Law & Exclusive Jurisdiction
This contract is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Any legal action, dispute, or proceeding arising under or in connection with this agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts at Surat, Gujarat.

## 3. Special Addendum Clauses

### Clause 19. For Taken-Over Sites (Handover Condition)
This maintenance contract is entered into on an "as-is" basis following a joint site inspection (Fire System Health Check-up) documented and signed by the handover representative and Northern Star Engineering's technician. NSE's maintenance obligation is strictly limited to equipment confirmed functional in that handover report. Defects noted at handover are the client's responsibility to rectify and will be quoted separately.

### Clause F. Complimentary First-Year AMC
Where the first year of Annual Maintenance is provided complimentary (₹0 AMC fee) as agreed (e.g., following a complete fire system project installation), all terms and conditions above apply in full. Renewal after the conclusion of the initial twelve (12) months is chargeable at prevailing commercial rates.
